HubSpot CMS Build & Relaunch

Sketch Development is a US-based software consultancy with strong brand guidelines and a clear design direction. They needed a HubSpot CMS developer who could match the quality of their design work — no compromise, no “close enough.”

Challenge

  • Detailed, opinionated designs that needed to be built faithfully on HubSpot CMS
  • Only a few pages had been designed — the rest needed to be created in the same visual language
  • Required both deep platform knowledge and genuine design judgement

Solution

  • Built a complete, custom HubSpot CMS theme — pixel-perfect to the original designs
  • Extended the visual language to create remaining pages, maintaining consistency across the site
  • Reusable components, flexible templates, and an intuitive editing experience for the marketing team
  • Full documentation and user guide so the team could manage content independently

Outcomes

  • Complete, production-ready website delivered in under 12 weeks
  • A flexible, yet guard-railed editing experience allowing for rapid, non-technical page building without breaking brand guidelines
  • Marketing team fully self-sufficient
  • New site up and running on schedule with minimal downtime, and zero major issues
