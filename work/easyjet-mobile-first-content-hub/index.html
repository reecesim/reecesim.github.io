Mobile-first Content Hub

A mobile-first, rich landing experience for a large travel brand.

Challenge

A large travel brand wanted to run a social media competition designed to convert their large following into marketing-ready contacts and direct bookings.

Solution

We designed and delivered a campaign landing page built around short-form video. Early concepts explored conventional scrolling layouts before evolving into a TikTok-style, swiping experience that mirrored the behaviour users were already in on social platforms, reducing friction.

Outcome

The campaign was an overwhelming success, capturing huge amounts of email sign-ups and validating the effectiveness of platform-native interaction patterns applied off-platform.
